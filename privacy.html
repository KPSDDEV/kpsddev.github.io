Privacy Policy for Nutcracker's Christmas

Effective date: 19 November 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Nutcracker's Christmas (the "game"), a mobile game made by KPSD, an independent developer ("we", "us"). If you have any questions, contact us at kpsd.dev@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store any personal information such as your name, email address, contacts or photos. The game does not need an account.

Your game progress (unlocked levels, stars, lives, achievements and settings) is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Some ads are optional rewarded videos you choose to watch (for example to get extra moves or a life), and some appear between levels.

To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • device type, operating system and app version
  • how you interact with ads (for example views and taps)

This information is handled by Google under its own policies. We do not receive information that identifies you personally. You can read how Google uses data from apps that show its ads at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Your choices and consent

  • Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent form when the game first opens. You can choose whether ads may be personalised. You can change your choice later from the game's Settings.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on the device).
  • Choosing not to have personalised ads does not stop ads from appearing, but they will be less tailored to you.

Children

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child's information has been collected through the ads in the game, contact us and we will help you reach the right service.

Your rights

If you live in the European Union or the UK, you have rights under the GDPR, including the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its processing. Because we do not hold personal data about you, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google, but you are always welcome to contact us and we will help. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Security and data retention

We do not run servers that store player data. Data held by Google AdMob is kept and protected according to Google's policies.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, for example because the game gains new features, we will publish the new version on this page and change the effective date at the top.
